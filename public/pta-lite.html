FAQ

Common questions

How is PTA Lite different from full-service Prime Team?

Full service is an ongoing retainer where we own your marketplace strategy, advertising and catalog day to day. Lite is the same team on a project basis — a defined deliverable, a fixed price, and a clear finish line. Many brands start with Lite and move to full service later; anything you spend on Lite in your first 90 days is credited toward a retainer if you do.

Do you guarantee reinstatement?

No one honestly can — the decision belongs to the marketplace. What we do guarantee is that we won't take a case we don't believe in, which is why every reinstatement starts with a free eligibility check. If we take your case, we keep drafting and escalating appeals until the account or listing is restored or the channel issues a final denial.

What's the turnaround on creative work?

Main image optimization runs about five business days, A+ Content seven to ten, and a full store build two to three weeks depending on page count. Timelines start once we have your assets and access, not at checkout.

Are you a law firm for trademark work?

Filings are prepared and submitted by a licensed US trademark attorney we partner with. Government filing fees are billed separately at cost. Prime Team Agency itself does not provide legal advice.

Which marketplaces do you cover?

Amazon, Walmart Marketplace and TikTok Shop for reinstatement work. Creative services cover Amazon and TikTok Shop; Walmart creative is available on request.

What if my situation doesn't fit a fixed scope?

Plenty don't. Multi-ASIN suspensions, section 3 violations with a linked-account component, or a store build with heavy custom photography usually need a custom quote. Reach out and we'll price it directly.
